Research Overview

The Metamorphosis Formula is a theoretical framework for analyzing structural and morphological transformations in language through mathematical formulations, grammatical algorithms, and chromatic representations.

The research integrates linguistic, mathematical, geometric, and visual approaches to examine the organization and transformation of grammatical information.

Theoretical Framework

  • Algorithmic Grammar
  • Grammatical Transformation Processes
  • Chromatic Algorithms and Grammatical Mapping
  • Grammatical Time, Tone, and Comparative Formulas
  • Geometric Architecture of Linguistic Information
  • Comparative Analysis of Romanian, Greek, and English
